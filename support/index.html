Support

Help for Block360 - Apps & Websites. Write to coolwithtech@gmail.com and we usually answer within two working days.

Blocking doesn't start

Open Block360 and check the Today screen. If it says Screen Time access is off, tap Turn on and confirm with Face ID or your passcode. Then choose the apps under "Apps that pause".

A website isn't blocked in Chrome or another browser

Website blocking works in Safari and in apps that open web pages inside them. To block another browser, choose that browser as an app.

Setting it up for my child

Install Block360 on your child's iPhone or iPad, signed in with your child's own Apple Account, and choose "My child". Your child must be in your Family Sharing group; Apple will ask you to approve with your Apple Account. After that, your child can't delete Block360 or turn it off without you.

I forgot the parent PIN

For safety the PIN can't be recovered. A parent can remove Block360 from the child's device (Apple asks for the parent's approval) and set it up again with a new PIN.

Restore Pro on a new device

Open Settings in Block360 and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing members get Pro automatically.

Cancel a subscription

In Block360, Settings > Manage subscription, or in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions.
