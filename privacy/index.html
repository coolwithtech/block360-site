Privacy Policy

Block360 - Apps & Websites for iPhone and iPad. Effective 11 October 2026.

Block360 is built to work with as little personal data as possible. We do not sell data and we do not show ads. Most of what the app knows stays on your device.

Screen Time data

Block360 uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). When you choose apps, categories or websites, Apple gives the app only opaque tokens, not app names. Your choices, schedules, limits, intention and counts of pauses are stored on your device in the app's private storage. The Screen Time report inside the app is drawn by Apple in a private extension; those numbers cannot leave the device, not even to us.

Child mode

A parent can set Block360 up on a child's device. Apple asks the parent to approve with their Apple Account through Family Sharing. The child's first name (optional) and the parent PIN stay on that device; the PIN is stored only as a salted hash. We do not collect personal information from children, have no accounts, and do not send a child's data anywhere.

Location (prayer times, optional)

If you turn on prayer-time mode and choose "Use my location once", the app asks for your location one time to work out prayer times. It keeps a rounded position (about 1 km) and the city name on the device and never sends it to us or anyone else. You can pick a city from a list instead.

Notifications

Notifications are created on your device (for example "Take your breath" or "Focus session done"). They are not sent through our servers.

Your choices

You can remove Screen Time access in iOS Settings > Screen Time, turn off notifications or location in iOS Settings, and delete everything the app stored by deleting the app (on a child's device, a parent's approval is needed).
