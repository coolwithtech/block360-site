What the app does

Block360 helps you limit your own use of apps and websites, or a parent limit a child's device, using Apple's Screen Time features. It is a tool for building habits, not a medical or therapeutic service. iOS lets the device owner remove Screen Time permissions in Settings, and website blocking applies to Safari and apps that use Apple's web views, so blocking cannot be guaranteed in every case.

Block360 Pro subscriptions

  • Pro is available as weekly, monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency before you buy.
  • The yearly plan may include a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly subscription starts and is charged to your Apple Account.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings.
  • Pro can be shared with up to five other members of your Family Sharing group.
  • Payments, refunds and billing are handled by Apple.
