Less scrolling, more living

Brakely blocks distracting apps and websites on iPhone and iPad, for yourself or for your child.

  • Choose the apps and websites that eat your time. Opening one first shows a short breathing pause; you choose to close it or go in for a few minutes.
  • A daily time budget turns the pause into a block until tomorrow.
  • Schedules for study, school, homework, bedtime, family dinner and prayer times, with an optional strict lock.
  • Focus sessions and a weekly report of the time you got back.
  • Child mode: set it up on your child's device with Family Sharing, protect settings with a parent PIN, and give extra time when they ask.
  • Private by design: Apple's Screen Time keeps your app choices and usage on your device. We never see which apps you use.

Brakely is a tool for building habits. It is not a medical treatment.
